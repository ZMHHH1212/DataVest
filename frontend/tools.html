What is the 50/30/20 rule?

A simple starting point for a budget: about half of take-home pay on needs (rent, food, transport, bills), 30% on wants (eating out, hobbies, trips) and 20% on saving and paying off debt. In an expensive city like Hong Kong, needs often take more than half. Then the savings share is what to protect, and wants are what to trim.
