01 / Philosophy

What we believe

Five ideas shape every page on DataVest. They are also a good way to think about money in general.

  1. 01

    Data before opinion

    Every claim on this site comes from real prices you can check. If the data doesn't support something, we don't say it.

  2. 02

    Say it plainly

    Finance is full of jargon. Each idea is explained in everyday words first, then given its proper name.

  3. 03

    Test, then trust

    Models are judged on data they never saw. When something fails, like the RSI signals on the Models page, we show that too.

  4. 04

    Risk is half the story

    Growth always comes with falls. Every market is shown with its worst drop and how bumpy the ride was, not just its return.

  5. 05

    Learning, not advice

    DataVest is free and sells nothing. It is here to help you think for yourself, not to tell you what to buy.
